Privacy Policy for Clip Sync

Effective Date: May 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Clip Sync ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, discloses, and protects your information when you use our TikTok integration tool (the "Service"). By using the Service, you agree to the terms of this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the Service.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information You Provide: TikTok account information (via TikTok Login Kit), profile information (open_id, display name, avatar), and any content you choose to process through the Service.
  • Automatically Collected Information: Usage data, log data (IP address, access times), and basic workflow execution metadata required for operation.
  • Information from TikTok API: When you authorize TikTok integration via OAuth 2.0, we access limited profile information (user.info.basic scope) and use the Content Posting API to upload videos as drafts. All data access is performed in accordance with TikTok's Developer Terms and Policies.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the Service's video processing and posting features
  • Process video content (transcription via Groq, caption generation via OpenRouter)
  • Respond to your requests and provide customer support
  • Monitor analytics and Service performance
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share your information with:

  • Service Providers: We use third-party services including Groq (for audio transcription) and OpenRouter (for AI caption generation). These providers process data solely on our behalf and only as necessary to deliver the Service.
  • TikTok: When you authorize the integration, data is shared with TikTok as required under their platform rules and your explicit consent.
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights.

4. Data Retention

We retain your data only as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes outlined in this policy. Video files are processed transiently and are not permanently stored. TikTok access tokens are stored while your integration remains active. You may disconnect TikTok access at any time via TikTok settings, which will revoke our access to your account.

5. Data Security

We implement reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your information. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for maintaining the security of your TikTok account credentials.

6. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to:

  • Disconnect TikTok access at any time via TikTok's settings page
  • Request access to or deletion of your data by contacting us
  • Opt out of non-essential data collection (where applicable)

7. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes by updating the "Effective Date" at the top of this page and/or through the Service.
